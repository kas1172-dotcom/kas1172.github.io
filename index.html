I'm a consultant, researcher, and writer based in Pittsburgh, PA — with deep roots in healthcare and a broader interest in how regulatory systems shape industries.

My path into this work wasn't a straight line. I started where most people do: wanting to help people directly. I spent years working in hospitals and shadowing physicians, genuinely pursuing a future in clinical medicine. But somewhere along the way I recognized something about myself — my strengths weren't at the bedside, they were in the room where decisions get made.

What I bring that's harder to find is the combination of operational experience and regulatory fluency — knowing how complex systems work from the inside and understanding the policy framework that governs them. I've navigated multi-jurisdiction compliance environments, built analytical frameworks from raw data, and translated technical findings into client strategy and board-ready recommendations. The domain is healthcare; the skills travel.

I'm preparing to take this further through graduate school, with a long-term focus on policy, regulatory strategy, and public interest advocacy. Outside of work you'll find me on a trail or planning my next trip somewhere new.

Healthcare Regulatory & Policy Monitor
Live · Weekly

Working in healthcare compliance, I spent a disproportionate amount of time just staying current — scanning OIG enforcement actions, Federal Register notices, new CMS rules, FDA actions, and congressional activity across a dozen sources, for a dozen clients. The manual overhead was real. I'd spend hours each week sorting through noise to find the two or three things that actually mattered to a specific client in a specific regulatory context.

So I built infrastructure to do that automatically. A pipeline pulls from 30+ government APIs and industry feeds every Monday, uses a language model to classify urgency and synthesize the implications for two distinct audiences — compliance and strategy professionals, and policy readers — and publishes a structured briefing as a static site. Zero manual effort after setup. The dashboard I wished had existed when I needed it most.
